## 1. Overview

CVP ("the Service") takes the protection of user privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains the information we collect and how we use it.

## 2. Information We Collect

As a desktop application, the Service processes the following information:

- **Local Files**: Image and video files that users directly open or process
- **Settings**: Application preferences (stored locally)

## 3. Use of Information

All collected information is processed only on the user's local computer and is not transmitted to external servers.

## 4. Information Sharing

The Service does not share user personal information with third parties.

## 5. Data Security

Since all data is processed in the user's local environment, data security depends on the user's system security.

## 6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through GitHub Issues.
